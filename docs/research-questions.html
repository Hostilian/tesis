Research questions & statistical validation

A focused consultation agenda connecting three existing thesis research questions with eight decisions needed before making empirical claims.

Research integrity: This project currently displays synthetic anomaly scenarios. Three public Sentinel-2 source previews demonstrate data acquisition, but no independently validated anomaly detections have been established. Historical model scores and economic correlations remain unverified.

The three research questions

RQ1 — Spatial anomaliesHow reliably can open satellite spectral APIs identify localized industrial and mining resource anomalies compared to ground truth records?
RQ2 — VIIRS and economicsWhat is the correlation between VIIRS night-time light fluctuations and macro/microeconomic indicators in the target regions?
RQ3 — Pipeline performanceWhat pipeline latency and processing constraints limit the scalability of free satellite APIs for real-time economic monitoring?

Current assessment: RQ1 and RQ2 remain empirically open; RQ3 is partially addressed by the software demonstrator but needs controlled workload measurements.

Eight questions for the statistics consultation

01

Ground-truth validation

How should I validate an unsupervised anomaly-detection model when independently verified real anomalies are scarce?

Compare independent field records, licensed reference datasets and blinded/manual labels. Agree on what counts as a true positive.

02

Sample size & independence

How many independent satellite observations, scenes, dates or field samples would support a defensible statistical claim?

Pixels in the same scene are spatially correlated; count independent sampling units and uncertainty.

03

Leakage-safe evaluation

How should I divide nearby pixels and consecutive dates into training, tuning and held-out testing sets?

Consider spatial block cross-validation, temporal holdout and leakage checks.

04

Simple baselines

Which simpler methods should Isolation Forest be compared with: seasonal Z-scores, change-point detection or threshold rules?

Specify baselines, parameters and selection criteria before inspecting test results.

05

VIIRS and economic trends

Is Pearson correlation appropriate for VIIRS night-time lights versus regional GDP, or should I adjust for trend, seasonality, autocorrelation and lag?

Define compatible temporal units, missing-data rules, lag selection and meaningful uncertainty.

06

Measurement artefacts

How can I distinguish genuine land-condition changes from clouds, seasonal vegetation, irrigation patterns, sensor calibration and measurement noise?

Request quality-control rules, sensitivity analyses and uncertainty representation.

07

Cross-resolution Portugal study

How should I compare Sentinel-1/2 observations with drone or aircraft imagery and field measurements when spatial resolutions and acquisition dates differ?

Select unit of analysis, aggregation, time windows and permissible cross-source comparison.

08

Minimum viable experiment

Given limited time and real ground truth, what is the smallest statistically credible empirical study for an Informatics bachelor's thesis?

Choose one region, one outcome, an independent benchmark, a simple baseline and reproducible holdout evaluation.

Potential Portugal / Alentejo application

Candidate — subject to supervisor approval

Could publicly available Sentinel-1/2 time series detect meaningful changes in agricultural land conditions in Alentejo, and does combining them with independent field measurements improve detection reliability?

Ground conditions, data licensing, field-to-device mapping, temporal alignment and spatial resolution must be checked before committing. Operational farm records, coordinates and private sensor data remain outside the public site.

Minimum decisions before claiming results

  • Choose one primary outcome, unit of analysis and independently verifiable reference data.
  • Define a spatial/temporal holdout and simple comparison baselines.
  • Specify sampling, handling of correlated observations, uncertainty and missing data.
  • For VIIRS–GDP, account for trend, seasonality, temporal aggregation and possible lags.
  • Decide whether available evidence supports empirical validation or only an engineering feasibility study.
  • Seek explicit thesis-supervisor approval before changing the original research scope.
