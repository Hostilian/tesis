Criteria for a validated research result

For each case study, obtain traceable source product IDs, acquisition windows, raster checksums and coordinate reference systems; process real optical/radar/VIIRS pixel values with documented atmospheric, cloud and temporal handling; record the ground-truth source and its independent spatial/temporal alignment; evaluate separate training and test regions; and publish reproducible score calculations with uncertainty and limitations.

Earlier draft figures such as F1 = 0.907 and Pearson r = 0.724 are not accepted as measured results until their original labeled sample, calculations and evaluation protocol are available and reviewed.
