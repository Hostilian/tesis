SOIL-5: five linked soil investigations

One reusable, laptop-scale Earth-observation evidence pipeline. Five independently falsifiable applications. Eight earlier candidates remain available for future research.

Research integrity: We have verified open Sentinel-2 pixel access and a two-date optical calculation, not soil sealing, erosion, sample savings, chemical concentrations, 90% laboratory agreement, or customer willingness to pay. This is not an approved change to the registered thesis.

The five active tracks

SOIL-01

EU Soil Evidence Pack

A reviewable case file with source scene IDs, dates, image quality, permitted source links, optional field references and clear uncertainty. Source proof; report design open

Buyer evidence: none. Competes with free Copernicus / QGIS reporting.

SOIL-02

Surface sealing and removal change audit

Compare real before/after imagery and flag optical change candidates for independent human checking. Two-date optical preview done

Zero independently verified sealing/removal cases. Existing Copernicus imperviousness products are the baseline.

SOIL-03

Soil sampling priority

Prioritize where in-person and laboratory sampling is most informative at a fixed sampling budget. No lab labels; evaluation pending

Benchmark against random, uniform grid and current professional practice; never claim reduced sampling without measurement.

SOIL-04

Bare-soil and erosion exposure

Flag changing vegetation cover or exposed surface using real optical features, rather than pretending to measure soil mass loss. Real indices; no erosion labels

Seasonal harvest, fire, vegetation and moisture can cause false positives.

SOIL-05

pH / nitrogen / phosphorus / potassium laboratory challenge

Explicitly test whether satellite data plus other covariates can predict any property within a meaningful independently verified tolerance. Not laboratory-equivalent

Optical satellites do not directly assay soil pH or nutrients. Genuine lab samples, independent regions and error limits are prerequisites.

Independent change truth

2

Actual soil laboratory samples

Only after lawful access: sample depth, date, method and real analyte values. Hold out entire sites or regions. No modelled SoilGrids values masquerading as ground truth.

Existing substitutes and buyers
