Evidence and privacy policy

Scope status

The formal assignment controls thesis scope. The current private Version 2 proposal seeks to preserve the registered aims and assess an agricultural workflow across satellite, existing authorized drone and crewed-aircraft imagery where relevant and feasible. This public page does not report supervisor approval, data acquisition or a completed cross-modal study. Course links are retained only when they have direct artifacts; weak fits are omitted.

Scientific status

Mock or synthetic examples demonstrate software behavior only. They are not observed events, independent labels or ground truth. Historical accuracy, correlation, spatial-statistics and performance figures are not presented as validated results until source inputs, configurations, run records, calculations and validation are available.

Required provenance

  • Provider, product and item identifiers, acquisition dates and license for each modality.
  • Study area, CRS, bands, resolution, quality masks and preprocessing.
  • Configuration, random seed, software versions, source revision, command, logs and output checksums.
  • Independent reference and a justified sampling/evaluation design for scientific metrics.

Privacy

The Pages artifact is a public status summary. It omits student numbers, direct contact details, private partner context, confidential imagery, private boundaries, credentials and raw sensor records. The site loads no third-party scripts or analytics.

Repository history, forks and cached copies can retain earlier versions. A current-page change does not erase historical copies.

Academic authorship

The formal assignment and current institutional policy control the thesis scope and AI-use boundary. The student author must produce and interpret their own results, discussion and conclusions from verified work.
